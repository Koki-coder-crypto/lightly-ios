PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This policy applies to FrameDrop, QR Keeper, Handy Print, Meeting Spark, Focus Pocket, Warranty Ledger, Leave Check, Receipt Split, Parcel Note, Wi-Fi Notes, Home Care, and Photo Cleaner: Review & Remove (the “Apps”).

Data collection

The Apps do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. They include no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and do not require an account.

On-device records

Entries, notes, QR code contents, PDFs, recordings, focus history, and Photo Cleaner analysis results are stored only on your device. Photo Cleaner accesses only the photos you authorize, never uploads them, and removes only the items you select and confirm; iOS moves them to Recently Deleted. Removing an App may remove its local records. You control any sharing you initiate from an App.

Device permissions

FrameDrop and Handy Print request photo access only after you choose photos. QR Keeper requests camera access only while scanning a QR code. Meeting Spark requests microphone access only when you start recording. A permission can be declined or changed in iOS Settings; functions requiring it will then be unavailable.

Purchases

Optional subscriptions and purchase restoration are handled by Apple through StoreKit. We do not receive your payment information.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact kouki_1203@icloud.com. Please do not send passwords, QR contents, recordings, or other private records.
