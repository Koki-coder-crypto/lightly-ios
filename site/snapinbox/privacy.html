SnapInbox Privacy Policy

Effective August 29, 2026

SnapInbox organizes photos and screenshots on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your photos, media metadata, review decisions, or purchase history. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking, or sell personal information.

Photo-library access is used only to show the items you choose to review and to perform an explicit action you confirm. SnapInbox never deletes media automatically. Apple processes App Store purchases and subscriptions under its own privacy policy.

For support or privacy questions, use the SnapInbox Support page.
