Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Data collection

SnapInbox does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, usage data, location data, or photos. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Photo Library access

SnapInbox requests access to your photo library only to let you review and organize screenshots on your device. Your photos are not uploaded or transmitted to us or to any third party.

Deletion

SnapInbox never removes photos automatically. You choose any item to delete, iOS asks for confirmation, and deleted items are handled by the system Photos app, including Recently Deleted where applicable.

Local records

App settings and your review progress are stored only in the app's local storage on your iPhone.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, visit our support page.
