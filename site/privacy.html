PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This policy applies to Lightly, FrameDrop, QR Keeper, Handy Print, Meeting Spark, and Focus Pocket (the “Apps”).

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information, photos, recordings, QR code contents, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data.

On-device processing

Each App processes its core content on your device. Selected photos, generated PDFs, saved QR codes, focus history, and recordings remain on your device unless you explicitly choose to share a file through iOS.

Permissions

Photo Library access is used only by FrameDrop and Handy Print for the photos you select. Camera access is used only by QR Keeper to scan a code. Microphone access is used only by Meeting Spark to record a memo. The Apps do not transmit this content to our servers.

Subscriptions

Optional Pro subscriptions and their management are handled by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit. We do not receive your payment-card information.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, visit Support. If this policy changes, this page will be updated.
