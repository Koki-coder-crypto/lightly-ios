Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Data collection

Caliqo does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, usage data, location data, or camera images. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Camera

The camera is used only to detect real-world surfaces and distances during AR measurement. Images and video are not saved or transmitted.

Local records

Saved measurement names, values, notes, and dates are stored only in the app's local storage on your iPhone.
