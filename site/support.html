Help for Lightly, FrameDrop, QR Keeper, Handy Print, Meeting Spark, and Focus Pocket.

Common questions

Where is my data?

Your photos, QR codes, PDFs, recordings, and focus history are stored on your device. The Apps do not use an account or cloud backup service.

How do I manage a Pro subscription?

Open Settings on your iPhone, select your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Apple manages purchases, renewal, cancellation, and refunds.

How do I remove content?

Use the in-app delete controls where available. FrameDrop only makes a review list; deletion remains under your control in Photos.

Contact
