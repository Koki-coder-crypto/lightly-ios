## Video constraints

- Use only SnapInbox simulator/physical-device recordings and original visual or audio assets.
- Do not promise storage reclaimed, duplicate detection, AI classification, or automatic deletion unless the released build demonstrably provides it.
- Keep the first problem statement within two seconds and one observable product action within six seconds.
