# VID-003 — The decision can wait

## Purpose

Test a calm, low-pressure screenshot-review hook for people who save screenshots as reminders. This is an original SnapInbox concept; it uses only a freshly recorded SnapInbox simulator/device session and original captions.

## 15-second vertical storyboard

| Time | Visual | Original on-screen caption | Narration / sound |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | A neutral Photos-style grid made from a SnapInbox test library, then SnapInbox opens. | `Saved it for later?` | One soft tap; no copied music. |
| 2–5s | SnapInbox's private/on-device onboarding card. | `Later needs an inbox.` | `A screenshot can wait without getting lost.` |
| 5–9s | Swipe Review: tap **Review later** on one test screenshot. | `Review later` | `Keep it out of your way until you're ready.` |
| 9–12s | Return to the calm inbox count; no deletion is shown. | `Nothing uploads. Nothing deletes itself.` | `You decide what happens next.` |
| 12–15s | SnapInbox icon and a real in-app view. | `Start a five-minute review.` | `SnapInbox is ready when you are.` |

## Post copy

Caption: `Screenshots are often reminders, not clutter. Give the decision a place to wait. SnapInbox keeps review on your device and never deletes anything automatically. Start a five-minute review from the link in profile.`
