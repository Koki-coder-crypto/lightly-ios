## 90-day gates

- Day 30: complete at least three experiments and diagnose the largest drop-off.
- Day 45: target at least 1% download-to-paid conversion; otherwise change the value proposition or free experience, not price.
- Day 60: target seven paid purchases per week; otherwise narrow the user problem before creating another app.
- Day 90: judge the goal by trailing-30-day proceeds after Apple commission and refunds.
