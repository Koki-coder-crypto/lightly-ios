## Store copy

### English

**Subtitle:** Review screenshots without the rush.

**Description:** SnapInbox is a private review space for the screenshots, long videos, and large photos you have not decided about yet. Choose a queue, review a small batch, then keep, defer, or remove only the items you select. Nothing is deleted automatically. Photo analysis and review choices stay on your iPhone; no account or advertising SDK is required.

**Keywords:** screenshot organizer,photo cleanup,declutter,camera roll,storage,review later

## Review notes

No login is required. Grant photo-library access, open the Screenshots queue, then keep, defer, or select items for removal. Any removal requires an explicit confirmation and moves items to Photos' Recently Deleted album; SnapInbox never deletes automatically or accesses another app's data. The annual subscription has a seven-day trial; the monthly plan has no trial. Restore Purchases is available on the paywall.
