## English descriptions

### FrameDrop

FrameDrop is a calm way to review a stack of photos. Pick a group from your library, decide one photo at a time, and add an optional reason for the photos you keep. FrameDrop never deletes your photos; you make the final change in Photos. Your decisions stay on your device.

### QR Keeper

QR Keeper saves the QR codes you need to come back to. Scan a code, add a label such as a gym, guest Wi‑Fi, or event check-in, and set an optional expiry date. Links never open automatically. Your saved codes stay on your device.

### Handy Print

Handy Print turns selected photos into a clean, single-page PDF. Choose a one, two, or four photo layout, add an optional note, then share the file with a printer, message, or another app. PDFs are made on your device.

### Meeting Spark

Meeting Spark keeps the meeting title, a short voice memo, and the next action together. Record after microphone permission, stop when you are done, and play your saved memo back later. Recordings are stored on your device.

### Focus Pocket

Focus Pocket is a simple timer for the task in front of you. Pick 15, 25, or 45 minutes, pause when life interrupts, and review completed sessions. There is no account and your history stays on your device.

### Photo Cleaner: Review & Remove

Photo Cleaner helps you review screenshots, long videos, and recent large photos without handing your library to a server. Choose a category, inspect each item, and select only what you no longer need. Before anything is removed, iOS asks for your confirmation and moves it to Recently Deleted. There is no account, automatic deletion, or cloud upload. Photo Cleaner does not clear iOS system storage or other apps’ data.

### Warranty Ledger

Warranty Ledger keeps return, warranty, and replacement deadlines in one local list. Add an item, choose its date, and see how many days remain. There is no account or cloud sync. The free plan stores up to 20 records; Warranty Ledger Pro unlocks unlimited records.

### Leave Check

Leave Check is a small checklist for the moment before you go. Add the things you need for a trip, commute, or errand, review the list, and remove entries when they are no longer useful. Your lists stay on your device. The free plan stores up to three lists; Leave Check Pro unlocks unlimited lists.

### Receipt Split

Receipt Split calculates a clear per-person share from a total amount and group size. Save the settlement locally so you can check it later. It does not connect to payment services or collect financial information. The free plan saves up to five settlements; Receipt Split Pro unlocks unlimited history.

### Parcel Note

Parcel Note keeps expected deliveries, pickup details, and short reminders together on your device. Add a parcel when you hear it is coming and remove it when it is complete. The free plan stores up to five notes; Parcel Note Pro unlocks unlimited notes.

### Wi-Fi Notes

Wi-Fi Notes helps you remember the connection details you choose for places you visit. Add a place and a note, then keep the record on your device. It does not scan networks or transmit saved information. The free plan stores up to 10 places; Wi-Fi Notes Pro unlocks unlimited records.

### Home Care

Home Care is a local list for home maintenance. Add a task, replacement date, and note for filters, appliances, and routine work. The free plan stores up to 10 maintenance records; Home Care Pro unlocks unlimited records.

## Review notes (English)

No sign-in is required. All primary functions are available without a subscription within a transparent monthly free allowance. Purchases are optional and unlock unlimited use. Photos, QR code data, PDFs, focus history, and recordings are processed and stored on-device. The review instructions in each app's `APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md` describe the exact flow.
